# Cricket-dashboard
🏏 IPL Analysis Dashboard (2008–2025)

An interactive Power BI report that breaks down 18 seasons of IPL history — teams, players, records, and season-wise standings — all in one clean, clickable dashboard.

📌 About the Project

The IPL Analysis Dashboard is a Power BI report built to explore Indian Premier League data from 2008 to 2025. It pulls together match stats, player records, and points tables season by season, so anyone — fans, analysts, or fantasy league players — can quickly see who's leading the race, who holds the records, and how each team has performed over the years.

🛠️ Tech Stack


Power BI Desktop – core tool used to build and design the report
Power Query – for cleaning and shaping the raw IPL data before loading it into the model
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – used for calculated measures like total points, cap holders, and dynamic season-based filtering
Data Modeling – relationships built between match data, player stats, and team data tables for smooth cross-filtering
File Format – .pbix for the working file, .png for dashboard previews


📊 Data Source

The dataset covers IPL seasons from 2008 to 2025, including match results, team standings, player performance (runs, wickets, fours, sixes), and venue information. Data was cleaned and structured in Power Query before being loaded into the model for analysis.

✨ Features & Highlights

Business Problem

With 18 years of IPL history, there's a lot of scattered data — team performances, individual records, season winners — and no easy way to look at it all in one place. Fans and analysts often have to dig through multiple sources just to answer simple questions like "who scored the most runs this season?" or "how did my team perform overall?"

Goal of the Dashboard

To build a single, interactive report that lets users:


Switch between seasons and instantly see updated stats
Track individual player records (Orange Cap, Purple Cap, most fours, most sixes)
Compare team performance through a live points table
Get a quick season summary — champion, runner-up, and key numbers — without digging through spreadsheets


Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Season Selector (Top Right)
A simple dropdown lets you pick any season from 2008 to 2025, and every visual on the dashboard updates instantly to reflect that year.

Champion & Runner-Up Cards
Right at the top, two cards highlight the season's winner and runner-up — for example, in 2025 it was Royal Challengers Bangalore as Champion and Punjab Kings as Runner-Up.

Key Stats Bar
A quick snapshot row showing total sixes, total fours, total matches played, total teams, centuries, half-centuries, and total venues used in the selected season.

Orange Cap & Purple Cap Stats
Two dedicated cards showing the season's top run-scorer (Orange Cap) and top wicket-taker (Purple Cap), along with their team and exact numbers — e.g., B Sai Sudharsan with 759 runs, and M Prasidh Krishna with 25 wickets in 2025.

Most Fours & Most Sixes
Player-level cards tracking the biggest boundary-hitters of the season, with player photo, team name, and total count.

Points Table
A full season standings table — played, won, lost, no result, tied, and total points for every team — sorted so the table-toppers are easy to spot at a glance.

Business Impact & Insights


Fan Engagement – Gives fans a fast, visual way to relive any IPL season without searching through old scorecards.
Performance Tracking – Helps analysts and fantasy league players quickly compare player and team form across seasons.
Historical Comparison – Makes it easy to spot trends, like which teams consistently make the points table top 4, or how individual records have evolved over the years.
Content & Media Use – Useful as a base for sports journalists or content creators needing quick, accurate season stats.
